## 1. Research and admission contract

Every inventory row has five obligations: an exact pin or an explicitly missing pin; its consuming specs; report **file and section**; rejected alternatives and a reason; and a concrete, release-tagged gate. A row covering several packages names every package and pin; its gate applies to each package. Conditional connectors and pilots remain in the inventory even when disabled.

Research status is independent of build status:

- **REPORT**: the named existing report compares the choice or its architecture. It does not certify the exact newer patch release.
- **UNRESEARCHED → PRIMARY-ADDED**: no adequate component-specific comparison was found in the report corpus. This revision supplies primary-source research in section 7, with a selection rationale and limits. The original gap stays visible.
- **UNRESEARCHED — OPEN**: the spec names a category without selecting a provider, parser, artifact, or supported contract. Research of known members does not certify an unspecified member. Its gate fails until a concrete selection and comparison are added.
- **PIN-MISSING**: a required version, source commit, artifact/model hash, build identifier, or versioned provider contract is absent. This always blocks building on that component. A service without software semver is pinned by API version where supplied, otherwise by a dated contract/schema snapshot, scopes, endpoint allowlist, and adapter release digest.
- **DEFERRED**: a conditional choice outside the enabled release. Deferral must be visible in capabilities and cannot count as passing a requirement that needs it.

**Common gate G0 [R0; repeated at the row's release]:** before implementing an adapter against a dependency, resolve its exact named artifact from the maintainer/vendor, record integrity hash, licence/use restrictions, release notes, supported host/runtime and a minimal contract probe in an isolated workspace or temporary database. For an API, record the version/header or dated schema digest, provider policy acceptance, least-privilege scopes and fixture source instead. No production credentials, account mutation, purchase, contact message, calendar invite, or provider notification is needed for this initial probe. A changed pin, schema, policy, or protected transport invalidates the affected evidence. Full installation/account evidence is a subsequent gate, never inferred from documentation.

Each gate result belongs in an integration-owned `specs/gates/` evidence file keyed by register ID, release, dependency digest, consuming-spec digest, timestamp, command/probe, expected/observed result and **PASS / FAIL / DEFERRED**. Failed/missing evidence prevents the dependent build card from starting; unrelated admitted cards can proceed. This task creates only this register, so it does not manufacture those future gate files.

Research jobs use the binding `toy-research` route: **`gpt-6.1-sol`, medium reasoning, Fast, mattpocock research skill**. A qualifying report retains requested and observed model/settings, supported ModelRoute version, loaded skill commit/hash, sources, alternatives, uncertainties and research-run receipt. This editing session has no such authenticated runtime receipt; its inline primary-source review must not be relabelled as an attested `toy-research` run. AT-RESEARCH-REGISTER-3 makes that distinction executable.

All Attention timing values, sleep rules, spacing, escalation and deadline offsets are referenced through **`behavior/attention.yaml`**, with the published policy digest in receipts. This register copies none of those values. Cadence consumes CONTRACTS’ authoritative full Sol/max/Fast review every 180 seconds including sleep; reduced settings are experimental R0 arms requiring explicit acceptance. Quota admission consumes Ops policy and the Ops policy; model selection consumes **`behavior/models.yaml`**. No registry introduces another timing, model, table, port, credential, or action owner.

Contract checks also distinguish immutable `ObservationEvidenceRef` (`life://observation/<UUID>`, verified UTF-8 byte spans) from typed `RecordRef`/receipt references; use the canonical `{ok:true,data:T}` / `{ok:false,error:{module,code,message,retryable,…}}` envelope. Device ingestion uses authenticated `(deviceId, streamId, sequence)` with decimal-string sequence and a committed owner receipt. Sources change delivery uses `sources.change_outbox` / `sources.change_receipts`, while external effects use `authority.outbox`. These are generated contract obligations, not new schemas or stores. **Gate [R1; expanded at R2/R4]:** cross-module success/error/evidence/device fixtures agree; stale Focus cannot clear quiet and stale stream/command generations cannot mutate a new incarnation.
